Allow microphone access?

Sound Fossil records only when you press the button. Recordings never leave your device and are never sent to a server.

  • Recording ends when you press Finish recording.
  • If Windows blocks the microphone, you may need to allow it in system settings.
  • Recordings stop automatically after 3 minutes.
